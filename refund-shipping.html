Shipping, Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Shipping

  • We deliver across India through courier partners. Shipping charges are already included in the product price. There is no extra shipping fee at checkout.
  • Orders are usually dispatched within 1–2 business days of confirmation, and delivery typically takes 3–7 business days depending on your location.
  • You can follow your order with Track order on our home page. Our courier partner may also contact you before delivery.
  • Please give a complete address and a working mobile number. We are not responsible for delay due to wrong details.

Cancellation

  • You can cancel an order before it is shipped by contacting us on WhatsApp +91 88844 69933. Once shipped, it cannot be cancelled.
  • If you paid online for a cancelled order, we refund the full amount.

Returns and refunds

  • We accept a return or replacement only for products that arrive damaged, leaking or incorrect.
  • Please report within 48 hours of delivery with your Order ID and clear photos or a video of the product and parcel, on WhatsApp +91 88844 69933 or at cleanmaxindiasupport@gmail.com.
  • After we verify, we send a replacement or refund. We do not accept returns of opened or used products, except for the reasons above.
  • Online payments: refunds go to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after approval. Cash on Delivery: refunds are sent to your bank account or UPI ID.
